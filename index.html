Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dynamics 365 Business Central?

Business Central is an ERP solution from Microsoft that helps small and medium businesses manage finance, operations, sales, and service.

How do I upgrade Business Central?

Upgrades are managed through extensions and automation tools. Best practice is to use AL development and follow Microsoft’s upgrade guidelines.
